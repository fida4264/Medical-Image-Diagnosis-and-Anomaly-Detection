## 5. Baseline Models Used
Standard transfer-learning models were deployed to provide a robust, reproducible benchmark:
- **Primary Backbone:** `ResNet-18` with ImageNet pretrained initial weights.
- **Comparative Architecture:** `EfficientNet-B0` (integrated into `training/v2_baseline/model_factory.py`).

## 6. Architecture & Classification Head Structure
The `V2BaselineModel` wraps the convolutional feature extractor:
```
Input Image (3, 224, 224)
   ↓
ResNet-18 Backbone (conv1 through layer4)
   ↓
AdaptiveAvgPool2d((1, 1)) -> Feature Dimension: 512
   ↓
Dropout(p=0.2)
   ↓
Linear(512, num_classes) -> Logits
   ↓
[Softmax: Multiclass/Binary]  OR  [Sigmoid: Multi-Label Retina]
```

## 7. Preprocessing
- Images resized to `(224, 224)`.
- Converted to 3-channel RGB (`.convert('RGB')`), cleanly preserving grayscale radiographs without distorting pixel distributions.
- Normalized using standard ImageNet channel statistics (`mean=[0.485, 0.456, 0.406]`, `std=[0.229, 0.224, 0.225]`).

## 8. Data Augmentation Policy
- **Validation & Test Sets:** Strictly **100% deterministic**. No random flipping, rotation, cropping, or color jitter.
- **Training Augmentation:** Conservative baseline augmentations to avoid distorting clinical pathology:
  * Horizontal flipping (`p=0.5`) on histopathology, skin, chest X-ray, ultrasound.
  * **No horizontal flipping** on Brain MRI or Brain CT (preserving anatomical laterality).
  * Mild affine: Rotation within ±8°, translation within ±4%, scale within [0.96, 1.04].
  * Mild color jitter: Brightness/contrast ±8% where appropriate.

## 9. Loss Functions
- **Multiclass:** `CrossEntropyLoss` (Brain MRI, Histopathology).
- **Weighted Multiclass:** `CrossEntropyLoss` with inverse frequency class weights (Chest X-ray, Skin Lesion, Ultrasound).
- **Multi-Label (Retina):** `BCEWithLogitsLoss` with per-label positive weights (`pos_weight`), outputting independent probabilities via sigmoid without softmax competition.

## 10. Class Imbalance Handling
- Class weights calculated on the training partition: `w_c = total_samples / (num_classes * count_c)`.
- Retina positive weights: `pos_weight_c = (num_negatives + eps) / (num_positives + eps)` (clipped to [0.1, 20.0]).
- Validation and test sets are **never artificially rebalanced** or altered.

## 11. Training Configuration & Hyperparameters
- **Global Seed:** `42` (deterministic random, numpy, torch, cudnn).
- **Optimizer:** `AdamW` (learning rate = `1e-4`, weight decay = `1e-4`).
- **LR Scheduler:** `CosineAnnealingLR` (`eta_min=1e-6`).
- **Batch Size:** `32`.
- **Early Stopping:** Patience = 5 epochs monitoring validation Macro-F1 / loss.
- **Gradient Clipping:** Max norm = 1.0.

## 25. Known Dataset & Methodological Limitations
1. **Absence of Patient Identifiers in Chest X-ray and Brain MRI:** Individual patient IDs were not released with the source datasets, precluding programmatic patient-level isolation (the original author splits were preserved to prevent leakage).
2. **Histopathology Tile Correlation:** SVS whole-slide image tile groups share micro-environmental context; tile-level grouping could not be enforced because parent slide IDs were omitted in the public dataset release.
3. **High Imbalance in Skin Lesions:** Melanocytic nevus constitutes >67% of cases while dermatofibroma represents <1.2%, naturally limiting unweighted recall on minor lesions.
4. **Retina Low-Prevalence Conditions:** Rare pathologies (e.g. hypertension retinopathy, 24 test cases) carry wider confidence intervals.
