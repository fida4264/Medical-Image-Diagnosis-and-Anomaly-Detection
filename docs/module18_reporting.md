## Overview

Module 18 introduces the **Medical Report Generation Engine** (`medical_ai_engine/reporting/`).
It synthesizes outputs from **`PredictionResult`**, **`ExplainabilityResult`**, and **`AnomalyResult`** into a professional clinical diagnostic report.

The engine requires zero modifications to the core prediction pipeline, operating downstream to format, contextualize, and export diagnostic findings in **JSON**, **Markdown**, and **PDF** formats.

## Core Capabilities

### 1. Multi-Signal Narrative Synthesis (`SummaryBuilder`)
Fuses diagnostic outputs into coherent clinical narratives:
- **Primary Diagnostic Finding**: Condition name and percentage confidence.
- **Diagnostic Impression**: Clinical severity assessment.
- **Explainability Narrative**: Visual saliency region coverage, intensity peak, and heatmap artifact paths.
- **Anomaly & OOD Narrative**: Training distribution status, Mahalanobis distance, and Unknown Disease alerts.

### 2. Clinical Action & Urgency Formulation (`RecommendationBuilder`)
Translates disease confidence, anomaly signals, and clinical risk levels into standardized recommendations:
- **Action Codes**: `URGENT_CLINICAL_REVIEW`, `ROUTINE_FOLLOW_UP`, `IMMEDIATE_TRIAGE`, `REPEAT_EXAMINATION_OR_SECOND_OPINION`.
- **Urgency Levels**: `EMERGENT`, `URGENT`, `ROUTINE`.
- **Actionable Steps**: Itemized follow-up procedures for attending clinical staff.

### 3. Multi-Format Exporters (`ReportExporter`)
- **JSON**: Machine-readable structured payload (`.json`) for EHR/EMR integration and API responses.
- **Markdown**: Formatted document (`.md`) suitable for web viewing, clinical notes, or documentation.
- **PDF**: Portable Document Format (`.pdf`) rendered via `ReportLab` (when installed) or built-in pure-Python PDF-1.4 binary engine.

### 4. Mandatory Clinical AI Disclaimer
Attached to every report:
> **DISCLAIMER:** This report is generated by an automated Medical AI Diagnostic & Anomaly Detection system. It is intended solely for clinical decision support and preliminary triage. This AI output does NOT constitute a final medical diagnosis or legal diagnostic opinion. All findings, risk assessments, and recommendations MUST be reviewed, validated, and signed off by a licensed healthcare professional before clinical action is taken.
